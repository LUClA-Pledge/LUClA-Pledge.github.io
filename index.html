About

I'm an Android developer working primarily in Kotlin, with a soft spot for Python scripts that glue things together. Most of my work is about turning clunky workflows into small, focused apps: local-first media tooling, LLM clients, and utilities that respect the user's data instead of locking it in.

Everything I ship is free and open source. If a tool of mine saved you time, that's the whole point.
